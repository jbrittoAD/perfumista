O que se sente

Creamy white gardenia: green-fruity tuberose-like top (Viridine/benzyl acetate) over a rosy PEA body with a soft sandalwood-vanilla base and indolic depth.

O que a combinação faz

Phenyl ethyl alcohol is the floral base; Methyl anthranilate + Indolene give the heady indolic-orange-flower gardenia signature; Viridine adds green hyacinth; Javanol/methyl laitone bring creamy sandalwood; heliotropine/ethyl vanillin soften.
