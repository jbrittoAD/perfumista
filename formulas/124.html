O que se sente

Fresh herbaceous lavender and bright bergamot up top, giving way to a sweet, warm, new-mown-hay heart of coumarin over a green, mossy, woody-musky drydown - the archetypal clean-yet-warm 'barbershop' masculine.

O que a combinação faz

The textbook fougere skeleton descended from Fougere Royale (1882). Its defining trick is the lavender-coumarin-oakmoss triad: aromatic lift of lavender/bergamot, sweet hay warmth of coumarin, green mossy earthiness of oakmoss form a self-contained three-point structure. No single fern smells like this - it is an abstract 'idea' accord, which is why it became the template for virtually all masculine perfumery. Poucher's no.1017 fleshes the triad out with florals, woods and animalic/musk fixatives for body and tenacity. NOTE: genuine published book formula with real parts (per 1000).
