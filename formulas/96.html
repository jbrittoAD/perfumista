O que se sente

A fruity-floral citrus for encapsulation: apple/pear fruity notes (Nectarate, Verdox, Manzanate), ionone-violet woodiness and a citrus-floral top; Bergamal gives a strong aldehydic citrus lift.

O que a combinação faz

Verdox (15.6) + Nectarate (15) + ionones over an ethylene-brassylate/Timbersilk musk base; Bergamal (7.5) delivers a pronounced citrus top designed to survive encapsulation (in IPM, not alcohol).
