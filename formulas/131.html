O que se sente

A cold, metallic-fresh burst - citrus and aromatic herbs over a soapy-clean lavender, wrapped in an airy sea-spray ozonic accord, drying to warm musky woods.

O que a combinação faz

Cool Water fused the classic lavender/coumarin fougere skeleton with a massive dihydromyrcenol overdose plus marine/ozonic materials, creating the aromatic aquatic. Dihydromyrcenol supplied a synthetic, near-metallic citrus freshness louder and longer-lasting than any natural citrus; pinning it to a fougere produced the template copied by hundreds of 1990s masculines.
