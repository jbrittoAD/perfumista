O que se sente

Powdery violet: the classic ionone violet-candy powder (alpha/beta ionone dominant) with a green violet-leaf top and a soft woody-heliotrope base.

O que a combinação faz

Alpha + beta ionone + Isoraldeine (methyl ionone) together (~45%) are the violet-powder core; Violet leaf absolute + methyl octine carbonate a green cucumber-leaf top; Heliotropine/anisic aldehyde powdery almond sweetness; Sandela/amyris a soft woody base. ~25% benzyl benzoate.
