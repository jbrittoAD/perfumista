O que se sente

A fresh, soapy, herbaceous-lavender opening flanked by tarragon and clary sage, developing a warm, sweet honey-tonka heart, and settling into a smoky, mossy, sophisticated drydown - airy and light for its era rather than dense.

O que a combinação faz

A textbook 1970s aromatic fougere (perfumer Jean Martel): it takes the classic lavender-coumarin(tonka)-oakmoss triad and layers an aromatic-herbal top of rosemary/clary sage/tarragon for freshness, then warms the base with honey, amber and tobacco to give a smoky-sensual signature distinguishing it from drier, greener contemporaries. The honey note is the genre-bending twist. NOTE: breakdown is a marketing/reviewer pyramid (Fragrantica/Osmoz), NOT a published formula - no authentic parts/ratios.
