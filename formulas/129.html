O que se sente

A big anise-and-lavender opening spiked with clary sage and citrus, over a spicy caraway/cardamom heart and a huge oakmoss-and-woody-musk base - polished, spicy, barbershop-modern.

O que a combinação faz

The reference modern aromatic fougere: keeps the classic lavender-coumarin-oakmoss skeleton but modernizes the top with dihydromyrcenol (its first landmark use) plus an anisic lavender pairing, giving a fresher, cleaner, more radiant lift than vintage fougeres. It became the blueprint bridging classic barbershop fougeres and the 1980s 'fresh' aromatic-fougere wave (Cool Water etc.). NOTE: documented percentages come from Ohloff/Kraft/Pickenhagen's Scent & Chemistry (2022); remaining notes marketing-derived.
