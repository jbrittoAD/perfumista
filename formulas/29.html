O que se sente

A juicy, crisp red/green apple fruit note wrapped in a soft floral-woody-musky base; fresh and fruity up top, rounded and clean in the drydown (built for rinse-off products).

O que a combinação faz

Fruity esters (manzanate, hexyl/ethyl/allyl esters, styrallyl acetate) build the apple; green notes (cis-3-hexenyl acetate, triplal) add stem/leaf realism; damascone/ionone give a natural fruity-rosy roundness; galaxolide + benzyl salicylate + Iso E Super + vertofix form a clean musky-woody fixative base that survives dilution in wash-off bases; DIPG is solvent.
