O que se sente

A twilight-blue, powdery floral-oriental: a strange medicinal anise-and-bergamot opening melting into waxy orange blossom and creamy rose, all wrapped in almond-vanilla heliotrope powder. Simultaneously cold/melancholic and warm/comforting - like violet-scented pastry and face powder.

O que a combinação faz

Defines the powdery semi-oriental by pairing an unusual anisic-aldehyde top with heliotropin's almond-vanilla powder, then floating soft white florals over the guerlinade base (vanillin + coumarin + tonka + iris + benzoin). The anise/heliotrope tension produces its bittersweet cold-yet-cozy twilight character; the guerlinade gives Guerlain family DNA.
