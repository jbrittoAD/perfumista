O que se sente

Author: made 'mostly with naturals to purposely recreate a vintage-type, dense-floral, yet powdery scent' that 'rests closer to the skin.'

O que a combinação faz

A pre-built Mousse de Saxe accord (geranium/anise/oakmoss/labdanum/civet), 1 g, is dropped into the base to give a dense powdery vintage character under a rose-jasmine-ylang floral heart; ionone + velvetone add powder, galaxolide/musks add persistence and skin-close diffusion.
