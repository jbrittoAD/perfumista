O que se sente

A sparkling lemon-citrus zest with a big fatty-aldehyde top and a rosy geraniol/citronellol heart; Bergamal boosts the aldehydic lemon brightness.

O que a combinação faz

d-Limonene (23) + Citronellol (17) + Linalool (14) with a strong C8-C12 aldehyde complex for lift; Bergamal sharpens the citrus top.
