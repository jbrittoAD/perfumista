O que se sente

Balsamic woods with spiced florals: benzyl-benzoate/hexyl-cinnamal floralcy, clove-cinnamon spice, musky Galaxolide and a vanillic-coumarinic sweet powdery drydown.

O que a combinação faz

Benzyl benzoate (48) + Timbersilk + Galaxolide form the woody-musky body; a spice complex (eugenol, cinnamics) and floral aldehydes give the spiced-floral character; Karmawood deepens the creamy woody base.
