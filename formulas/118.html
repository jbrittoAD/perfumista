O que se sente

A dark, dense spicy-resinous oriental: a huge clove/carnation (eugenol) spice blast over indolic jasmine, drying into smoky myrrh-opoponax-incense and sweet benzoin-vanilla-labdanum amber. Powerful, warm, narcotic, tenacious.

O que a combinação faz

The definitive spicy oriental and a direct, amplified reworking of Youth Dew: the mellis accord (benzyl salicylate + eugenol/clove + patchouli + hydroxycitronellal + coumarin + Vertofix, per Calkin & Jellinek) over a stack of balsamic resins - benzoin, styrax, opoponax, myrrh, tolu - plus vanilla and labdanum. Salicylates and aldehydes lift and project the massive eugenol spice while the resin base fixes it and adds smoky-sweet depth. CAVEAT: mellis percentages are the GENERIC accord from Calkin & Jellinek, NOT YSL's bench formula; full pyramid is marketing (Fragrantica/Wikipedia). Parts null.
