O que se sente

A juicy mandarin/citrus burst: distilled citrus and citral over a big dihydromyrcenol freshness, with green-fruity and slightly minty facets and a light musk base.

O que a combinação faz

Dihydro myrcenol (26) + Citrus oil distilled (10) with citral and aldehydes; Bergamal adds an aldehydic citrus sparkle. ~29% DPG.
