O que se sente

A bitter, cut-stem green opening (galbanum) over a lush indolic floral heart, drying to a dark, mossy, earthy-ambery chypre floor. Elegant, austere, unmistakably green-mossy.

O que a combinação faz

Defines the postwar green floral chypre by grafting the sharp galbanum/green-aldehyde top of Vent Vert and Ma Griffe onto the classical Chypre-de-Coty skeleton (bergamot over oakmoss-patchouli-labdanum). The green top gives bracing contrast against the warm mossy animalic base, and the floral heart bridges them. CAVEAT: partial - a documented structural breakdown, not a bench formula. A single figure circulates (core chypre accord cited ~60%) but is secondary/unverified; all proportions null. Pyramid marketing-derived.
