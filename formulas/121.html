O que se sente

A cool, soapy, sparkling 'champagne' fizz of aldehydes bursting over a creamy abstract floral bouquet (jasmine-rose-ylang-iris), settling into a warm powdery sandalwood-musk-vanilla drydown. Famously 'not a flower' but the abstraction of many.

O que a combinação faz

Defines the floral aldehydic genre: an overdose of aliphatic aldehydes C-10/C-11/C-12 (~1% total - huge for 1921) laid over a rich natural rose-jasmine-ylang-iris bouquet. The aldehydes add a fatty, waxy, citrus-soapy diffusive 'lift' and abstraction that detaches the scent from any single flower, while methyl ionone + ylang + hydroxycitronellal build the floral body and coumarin/vanillin/musks give the warm base. That aldehyde-over-bouquet accord is the template copied by Arpege, Madame Rochas, Rive Gauche, White Linen. NOTE: percentages are Calkin & Jellinek's published analysis, not Chanel's formula.
